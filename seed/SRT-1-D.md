# SRT-1 · AMENDMENT D — The Non-Reduction Clause

> **CONSTITUTIONAL TEXT. IMMUTABLE.** Adopted 2026-08-31.
> Spoken by the creator. Preserved verbatim.
>
> **Standing:** Controls over every UI, reorganization, prototype and
> "simplification" proposal in this corpus, Amendment C included. Where any
> organizing change would reduce the depth of an existing room, panel or
> workstation, this amendment stops it.

## What this establishes

*(Framing added for enforcement. The words above are the law.)*

**D.i — Organizing is not replacing.**
The objective is the creator's *process*, not the creator's *tools*. Any change
that improves navigation by removing capability has failed, however much calmer
it looks.

**D.ii — The organizing layer never enters a room.**
It decides which room the creator is in and attaches intelligence at the edges.
Inside a room, the existing workspace renders exactly as built. Progressive
disclosure operates BETWEEN compartments, never INSIDE one.

**D.iii — A summary of a room is never a version of a room.**
A prototype card standing where a workspace belongs is scaffolding and must be
labelled as such. It must never be proposed, merged, or read as the room.

**D.iv — Depth already earned is not up for renegotiation.**
The Mix room, the Master room, Write & Record, the side-panel drawers and
modals, and every specialist workstation are settled. They may be extended.
They may not be thinned to make room for anything, the connective intelligence
included.
