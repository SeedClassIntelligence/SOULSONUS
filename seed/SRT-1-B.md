## What this establishes

*(Framing added for enforcement. The words above are the law; the words below
are only the mechanism for checking it.)*

**B.i — The creator's felt perception is a valid instrument, not deficient data.**
It does not require conversion into a technical measurement before it counts.
A creator can act on it and can tell when a thing is fixed. Any part of this
system that treats unformalized human knowing as a problem to be solved has
reproduced the exact failure SoulSonus exists to correct — the DAW telling a
musician their humming is not music until it becomes MIDI.

**B.ii — The measure of the system is fidelity of relay.**
Not technical correctness of output. Not conformance to loudness, timing, or
preservation targets. Those measure the machine against itself. The only
measurement that settles whether SoulSonus worked is whether what came out
matches what the creator carried inside. Every other metric is subordinate.

**B.iii — "That's not what I heard" is the highest-value signal in the session.**
It is the creator's instrument reporting a gap in the relay. It must be
recordable in the creator's own words, attributable, addressable in
conversation, and retained. It is not a reject button. A rejection stored as one
bit discards the most informative thing the creator produced.

**B.iv — The gap is a conversation, not a verdict.**
The creator must be able to say it to the co-producer or the engineer, be
answered, and go back and forth until the relay is right. A one-shot
accept/discard is not this. The exchange itself is session state and survives.

**B.v — The name is the specification.**
*Soul* — what the creator carries inside. *Sonus* — its accurate sonic
realization. The product is the passage between them. Anything that does not
serve that passage is not SoulSonus, however well engineered.
