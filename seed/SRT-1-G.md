## What this establishes

*(Framing added for enforcement. The words above are the law.)*

**G.i - Nothing is removed for purity.**
A capability is not deleted because it is not how the owner works. Text
prompting stays. Voice stays. Either one is a legitimate way in. The design
question is never whether to allow a capability -- it is where that capability
belongs.

**G.ii - The owner is not the only user.**
This is built from the owner's creative understanding, and it will be used by
people who work differently. A platform that only serves its author is a
personal tool. Both must be true at once: the owner's way is the spine, and
other ways are properly placed around it.

**G.iii - Proper place is decided by what the capability is anchored to.**
Of the six E05 tasks, `text2music` is the only one that requires no source
audio and the only one that is not duration-locked -- the contract's own words
are that it "invents a length, so its result cannot be aligned by construction
and has to be placed deliberately." That is the technical fact that decides
its place: it produces material with no relationship to anything the creator
performed. Every other task is anchored to their audio.

So text2music belongs where there is nothing yet -- an empty session, a
creator with no take, no hum, no beatbox. The moment a performance exists in
the session, the anchored tasks are the right ones, because they preserve it.

**G.iv - Placement is not hierarchy of worth, it is hierarchy of anchoring.**
Material that came from a text prompt is not lesser. It is unanchored, and it
must be labelled as AI-originated in provenance (clause XVIII.3) so the
creator always knows which parts of a project came from them and which did
not. That labelling is what makes offering it honest rather than dilutive.
