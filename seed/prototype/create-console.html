What this screen is

The reconciliation specified in seed/SRT-1-A.md and seed/SRT-1-C.md: the existing build supplies the functional body, the reclamation supplies the cognitive nervous system. Nothing was removed to achieve calm — every workstation, tool, section control and professional surface is still on this screen. What changed is that they now sit at a declared disclosure level instead of all competing for attention at once.

The four things that are genuinely new: the Live Expression Engine (beatbox demoted from defining the system to being one of seven modalities), the Interpretation layer that shows what SoulSonus believes a performance means before anything is realized, the Creative Intent model that makes preservation and permission visible, and the ChangeSet contract that declares what will not change and writes a new revision rather than overwriting your work.

The fifth is the one underneath all of it. Reject the proposal and the system does not discard a candidate — it asks what you heard, in your words, and keeps going. Soul is what you carry inside; Sonus is its accurate realization; the product is the passage between them, and the only measurement that settles whether it worked is yours.
