# SRT-1 - AMENDMENT F - Capture Is Sacred, Interpretation Is Revisable

> **CONSTITUTIONAL TEXT. IMMUTABLE.** Adopted 2026-09-01.
> Spoken by the creator. Preserved verbatim.
>
> **Standing:** Governs the entire capture path. No clause, feature, panel or
> optimisation may cause a performed sound to be lost, held, gated or
> silently discarded.

## The creator's words

> The goal is to always try to maintain what the system hears. So if the
> system hears a boom cat this time and the third, then... yeah. But that's
> why we have the instruments where we can add or we can ask the co-producer
> to fatten that up, to clean it up, things of this nature. But the goal is
> always to maintain the original seed's concept... everything that we pasted
> in regards to the original understanding of the platform, we are to capture
> that, still distribute it to its respective tracks, but then also give it
> the ability to edit it, alter it, work with it, enhance it, expand it.

## What this establishes

*(Framing added for enforcement. The words above are the law.)*

**F.i - Capture is unconditional.**
What the system hears is written. Not held pending a question, not gated on
confidence, not dropped because a condition was unmet. A performance that
reached the microphone reaches a track.

**F.ii - Distribution is immediate.**
Heard material separates onto its respective tracks as it always has. When no
existing channel can host a sound type, a channel is created for it. Nothing
waits for the creator to decide where it goes.

**F.iii - Misclassification is not failure. It is a first draft.**
If the system hears "boom cat" where the creator meant something else, that
is an acceptable outcome, because the correction happens afterwards through
the instruments and the co-producer -- fatten it, clean it, replace it, move
it. The system is not required to be right on the first pass. It is required
never to lose the pass.

**F.iv - Everything captured is editable, alterable, enhanceable, expandable.**
Re-interpretation is a permanent affordance on all captured material, from
every modality and every input, not a prompt shown once at capture time.

**F.v - Interpretation never blocks capture.**
Any panel, hypothesis, confidence reading or role choice is offered after the
material is committed and safe. Ignoring it entirely leaves the take exactly
as performed.
